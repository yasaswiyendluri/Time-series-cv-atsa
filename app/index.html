METHODOLOGICAL ADVANCE

Continuous Joint Selection Criterion ($\mathcal{J}_\lambda$)

Bergmeir et al. (Section 6) used an ad-hoc 2-step heuristic: first filter candidate models by Ljung-Box $p > 0.05$, then select the minimum CV error. We formulate a mathematically unified, continuous Pareto objective:

$$\mathcal{J}_\lambda(M) = \text{CV-RMSE}(M) + \lambda \cdot \max\bigl(0,\, -\log_{10}(p_{\text{LB}}(M)) - 1.301\bigr)$$
